### Requirements not met

- None. Every requirement R1 to R10 is met on paper or by design.
- Thin margin: at 30 m with the 40 mm main the two pump belts carry 92 % of their allowable pull. The 40 mm main is limited to 30 m; deeper shafts use the 32 and 25 mm mains.
- R4 (useful flow from 60 m) is met only with the 25 mm main, a different pipe from the priced 30 m kit.

### Build plan findings

- The concept's single welded frame (over 100 kg, 3.9 m) could not be carried to a remote collar: split into two bolted modules, the heavier 69.6 kg.
- The concept bore on the collar lip: moved onto staked sleepers set at least 0.35 m back.
- One pump belt would have carried about 184 % of its allowable pull at 30 m: two matched belts.
- Undefined clutch, bearings, pipe head, guide block, duct head, plates and guards were designed as makeable parts; a backstop was added; every fixing has a crossbar under it.
- Assembly order is fixed by the shaft: guide block, main and rope are threaded on the ground and lowered before the pipe head plate is clamped; the hood goes on last over the wheel.
- 23 making sketches, 11 joint close-ups, 13 step pictures; `drawing.py --check-text` passes on every sheet and the concept blueprint.

### Safety concerns

- Carbon monoxide: the exhaust riser is 6.46 m from the blower intake. A wind blowing from the riser toward the frame could still carry exhaust to the intake; the frame is set out with the riser downwind, and an intake CO check in varied winds is a first check (CLD-BLD-001 section 5). The 6 m minimum stays until that evidence exists.
- The gas detector is the control that matters below ground; ventilation alone does not make a shaft safe.
- Falls: the collar is an open drop during installation. The build plan requires the collar fenced, harnesses within 1 m of the edge, and nobody on the frame (safety stops S2 to S4).
- Moving machinery: belts, shafts, the wheel and the rope. Full guarding, a backstop and an engine-stopped rule (S5, S6).
- Lifting: the span module is 69.6 kg, a four-person lift over an opening (S3).
- Zinc fume when welding galvanised exhaust parts (S1).
